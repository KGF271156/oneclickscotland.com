The story so far

What is a clan?

Clans were the kin-groups of the Highlands and Borders, each with a chief, a territory and a name. Many families also belong to a clan through a 'sept' surname linked to the main name.

Tartan

Woven patterns linked with families, districts and organisations. The Scottish Register of Tartans, held by the National Records of Scotland, records registered designs.

1746 and after

After Culloden, the Dress Act banned Highland dress. It was repealed in 1782, and tartan became a worldwide emblem of Scottish identity in the 19th century.

Chiefs and the Lyon Court

The Court of the Lord Lyon, Scotland's heraldic authority, deals with chiefs, coats of arms and clan matters.

Clan societies abroad

Clan societies in North America, Australia and New Zealand hold gatherings and family research, and often attend the Highland games.

Tartan Day

Tartan Day, on 6 April, is marked in Canada and the United States, the date of the Declaration of Arbroath of 1320.
