What Scotland watches

Football

Celtic and Rangers meet in the Old Firm derby, Edinburgh has Hearts and Hibs, and Aberdeen and Dundee have their own proud rivalries. The Tartan Army follows the national team around the world.

Rugby union

Scotland play at Murrayfield in the Six Nations; the annual match with England is for the Calcutta Cup.

Golf

Scotland is the home of golf, and St Andrews, Carnoustie, Muirfield, Troon and Turnberry are among the courses that have hosted the Open.

Shinty

An ancient Gaelic stick-and-ball game, still played in the Highlands and islands, and the Camanachd Cup final is its big day.

Highland Games & curling

Caber toss, hammer and stone put are the heavy events; curling is a game with deep Scottish roots and an Olympic history.
