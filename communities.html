Communities built to last

Caledonian & St Andrew's societies

Long-established societies that hold dinners, ceilidhs and charity events, often marking St Andrew's Day and Burns Night.

Burns clubs

Clubs that hold Burns suppers every January and keep the poet's songs and verse alive.

Pipe bands & Highland dancing

Bands, schools of piping and Highland dance teams, many with competitions at Highland games abroad.

Clan societies

Associations for people sharing a clan name, with gatherings, newsletters and family research.

Gaelic learners & Scots speakers

Classes, choirs and mòds for people keeping Scottish Gaelic and Scots going abroad.

Supporters & newcomers

Football and rugby supporters' clubs, and networks for Scots who have moved recently for work.
