Throughout the year

National dates worth knowing

Burns Night: 25 January

The poet's birthday, marked with haggis, neeps and tatties, whisky, and the Address to a Haggis.

Edinburgh in August

The Fringe, the International Festival and the Royal Edinburgh Military Tattoo fill the capital.

Highland games: summer

From Braemar and Cowal to Inverness, caber tossing, hill racing and piping run from May to September.

St Andrew's Day: 30 November

Scotland's national day, a public holiday, marked with ceilidhs and dinners around the world.

Hogmanay: 31 December

Scotland's New Year, with street parties, fireworks and first-footing at midnight.
