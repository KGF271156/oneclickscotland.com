Planning the trip

When to go

May to September has the longest days and the best chance of dry weather; August is busy in Edinburgh for the festivals. Autumn is good for colour in the glens, and winter suits Hogmanay and the ski centres.

Getting around

Trains link Edinburgh, Glasgow, Aberdeen and Inverness, and ferries serve the islands. A car helps in the Highlands, but many roads are single-track, so allow extra time.

Off the main circuit

The Borders, Dumfries and Galloway, Angus and the Outer Hebrides are quieter than the Royal Mile or Loch Ness.

Roots trips

If you're tracing family, plan the records first, then add the glen, the parish church and the island ferry.
